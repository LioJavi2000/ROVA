Our Story

We got tired of gear
that didn't work.

So we built something that does.

Rova started with a simple frustration. Three harnesses that slipped. Two leashes that snapped. A bowl our dog ate around in six seconds.

We're not outdoor performance athletes. We're not veterinarians. We're dog owners who got fed up with products that looked good on Amazon and fell apart on day two.

Every Rova product starts with a real problem and ends with a solution that actually holds up. No celebrity endorsements, no subscription boxes, no proprietary technology that's just marketing copy for basic physics.

Just gear that works, sized correctly, priced fairly, backed by a team that picks up the phone.

Function First

We don't launch a product unless it solves a specific problem better than what's already out there. If we can't point to the failure we're fixing, it doesn't ship.

Real Fit

Size guides built around actual dog measurements — not generic small/medium/large buckets. Free exchange if we get it wrong. No interrogation, no return labels in tiny print.

Built to Last

No cheap materials. No planned obsolescence. We use the same hardware on a $52 harness that costs three times that from other brands. If it breaks, we want to hear about it.

Built for real dogs.
For owners who've been let down before.
